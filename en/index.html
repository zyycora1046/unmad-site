03 / FAQ

A few things you might wonder

Is it free?+

The core features are free, with no ads or subscription. An optional tip buys the team a milk tea and unlocks nothing.

Do I need an account?+

No sign-up, no login, and no internet connection needed. Just open it.

Can my draft get sent to anyone?+

No. Unmad has no send button. When a cool-down ends, you simply get to read your draft again.

Is there an Android version?+

It’s an iPhone app for now. You can switch between English and Simplified Chinese in the app.
